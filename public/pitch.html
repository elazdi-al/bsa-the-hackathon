Any two sign.

One threshold signing key. No complete key stored anywhere.

swissquote wallet05 / Recovery

Lost phone.
Same wallet.

Lock bank-assisted payments from e-banking or support.

Alert and allow cancellation

Keep the Safe address and balances. Invalidate the old shares.

swissquote walletAppendix / Tradeoffs

What we need to validate.

Recovery has limits

Losing both the device and recovery passkey can mean permanent loss. Offer a second recovery passkey and regular checks.

Checks have a boundary

The bank's rules cover assisted signing. Independent mode preserves client control and bypasses those rules.

Production needs review

Audit the signing and recovery design. Confirm licensing, regulatory treatment and ownership verification.

First prove create, sign, recover and exit. Then test with clients.
